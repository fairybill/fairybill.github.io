# Privacy Policy — FairyBill

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026  
**Contact:** [spliteasyone@gmail.com](mailto:spliteasyone@gmail.com)

FairyBill (“we”, “the app”) helps households organize bills recognized from email. This policy describes what the app does on your device and what we do not do.

## Summary

- Bill data and mail access tokens are stored **only on your device**, not on our servers.
- We read Gmail **only** to find and download messages related to bills and (if you enable it) Interac e-Transfer notifications.
- We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use Gmail data for advertising.

## Information the app processes

When you connect Gmail, the app may process:

- Email metadata and content needed to detect bills (sender, subject, date, body).
- OAuth tokens issued by Google to access Gmail on your behalf.
- Names you assign to family mailboxes, bill amounts, dates, and categories derived from email.

## Where data is stored

All of the above stays in the app’s local encrypted storage on your phone and is not included in iCloud or computer backups. Disconnecting a mailbox or deleting the app removes that data from the device. Gmail sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only; disconnecting revokes them, and if you delete the app instead, you can remove FairyBill’s access in your Google Account (a later reinstall also revokes any token left behind).

## How we use Gmail data

Use is limited to:

1. Searching and reading messages that appear to be bills or e-Transfer notifications (when enabled).
2. Extracting bill fields to show summaries and trends in the app.

We do not use Gmail data to train general AI models, for ads, or for unrelated products.

## Sharing

We do not share Gmail or bill data with third parties except:

- **Google**, when you use Sign in with Google / Gmail API under Google’s terms.
- **Service providers** you choose (e.g. email provider) when the app calls their APIs with your authorization.

## Your choices

- You can use the sample mailbox without connecting Gmail.
- You can disconnect mailboxes and delete local data in Settings.
- You can revoke FairyBill’s access in your [Google Account security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Children

FairyBill is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change; continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
